## 1. Constitutional Preamble & Strategic Progression

Milestones N1–N22 established:
- **N1–N16**: Governed, institutional-grade AI Learning Operating System.
- **N17**: Continuous Evolution, Research & Governance OS.
- **N18**: Advanced Learning Science, Cognitive Personalization & Human Development.
- **N19**: Global Skills, Evidence & Credential Network.
- **N20**: Lifelong Learning OS & Human Capability Graph.
- **N21**: Global Learning & Human Capability Ecosystem Intelligence.
- **N22**: Global Human Capability Exchange, Lifelong Opportunity Network & Trusted Learning Economy.

Milestone N23 advances the platform into **Human Capability Intelligence, Global Learning Mobility & Collective Human Potential**:
$$\text{LEARN} \to \text{PRACTICE} \to \text{DEMONSTRATE} \to \text{VALIDATE} \to \text{BUILD CAPABILITY} \to \text{SHARE} \to \text{TEACH/MENTOR} \to \text{CONTRIBUTE} \to \text{COLLABORATE} \to \text{APPLY} \to \text{CREATE EXPERIENCE} \to \text{GENERATE NEW EVIDENCE} \to \text{REFLECT} \to \text{RELEARN}$$

### The Sovereign Principle
**YOUVA may help humans understand, develop, demonstrate, transfer and apply capability. It must never determine human worth, destiny, employability, social value, or the "optimal" life of an individual.**

## 2. Foundational Constitutional Invariants

### Invariant 1: Core Epistemic Triad (Clause N23.4)
$$\mathbf{\text{Observed Evidence} > \text{Inferred State}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Demonstrated Capability} > \text{Predicted Potential}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Human Choice} > \text{Algorithmic Optimization}}$$

### Invariant 2: Capability Is Not Potential (Clause N23.5 & N23.6)
$$\mathbf{\text{Demonstrated Capability} \ne \text{Predicted Potential} \ne \text{Destiny} \ne \text{Human Worth}}$$
1. The platform records what a learner has demonstrated; it is constitutionally prohibited from assigning permanent potential labels, such as:
   - *"Your maximum potential is X."*
   - *"You are naturally suited for Y."*
   - *"You are a low-potential learner."*
2. System language must remain exploratory and evidence-grounded:
   - *"Here are capabilities you have demonstrated."*
   - *"Here are areas you may choose to explore."*
   - *"Here is additional evidence that could clarify this capability."*

### Invariant 3: Strict Identity Partitioning & No Surveillance Graph (Clauses N23.11, N23.80)
$$\mathbf{\text{Human Identity} \ne \text{Learning Record} \ne \text{Evidence} \ne \text{Credential} \ne \text{Capability} \ne \text{Opportunity History}}$$
1. The Global Capability Graph must never become a centralized human surveillance registry or social-credit apparatus.
2. Learners own their **Portable Human Capability Identity** and retain absolute sovereignty over with whom, for what purpose, and for how long data is shared.
3. No probabilistic identity guessing: cross-institution records cannot be merged based on speculative algorithmic similarity.

### Invariant 4: No False Equivalence in Capability Translation (Clauses N23.15–N23.20)
1. Two capabilities with similar titles are not automatically equivalent across different institutions.
2. Mappings require explicit scope, level, evidence requirements, statistical confidence, human review status, and versioning.
3. Capability progression levels (`FOUNDATIONAL`, `DEVELOPING`, `DEMONSTRATED`, `VALIDATED`, `APPLIED`, `ADVANCED`) represent empirical evidence states, never innate human rank.
4. Cross-domain capability transfer must be demonstrated through empirical transfer tasks, not assumed.

### Invariant 5: No Automatic Institutional Recognition (Clauses N23.58–N23.64)
1. YOUVA provides portable, verifiable capability evidence and semantic translation.
2. Recognition, credit award, or advanced standing remains the sovereign prerogative of the receiving institution. YOUVA can facilitate evidence exchange; it cannot force institutional recognition or rewrite original credentials.

### Invariant 6: Attributable Contribution Without Social Ranking (Clauses N23.26–N23.29, N23.93–N23.95)
1. Contribution tracking (mentoring, peer feedback, OER, code, research) must not evaluate human worth via popularity metrics (likes, views, follower counts).
2. Team project attribution must preserve individual roles without false equalization or speculative AI percentage slicing.
3. Material AI contributions to knowledge or educational artifacts must be transparently disclosed.

### Invariant 7: Human Decision Sovereignty & Non-Negotiable AI Boundaries (Clauses N23.32, N23.190–N23.197)
1. AI acts as an organizer, translator, recommender, and explainer. Where validated human expertise exists, AI must not supplant it.
2. AI agents cannot autonomously negotiate consequential commitments, accept binding agreements, or make life decisions.
3. Autonomous AI actions require explicit, least-privileged, revocable learner authorization tokens.

### Invariant 8: AI Removal Test & Non-Punitive Agency Evaluation (Clauses N23.154–N23.159)
1. The system evaluates whether a learner can perform tasks when AI assistance is removed, distinguishing independent capability from assisted execution.
2. AI dependency monitoring provides scaffold reduction and metacognitive reflection; creating permanent punitive "AI dependency" scores is strictly barred.

### Invariant 9: Scoped Safety Kill-Switches & Emergency Containment (Clauses N23.185–N23.189)
The platform maintains independently operable kill-switches for:
- `OPPORTUNITY_NETWORK`
- `COMMUNITY_FEATURES`
- `EVIDENCE_EXCHANGE`
- `EXTERNAL_INTEGRATIONS`
- `AI_RECOMMENDATIONS`
- `GLOBAL_EMERGENCY_SHUTDOWN` (strictly governed and auditable).

## 3. The 7 Progression Evidence States

Milestone N23 models capability development as evidence states rather than innate human traits:
1. `FOUNDATIONAL`: Initial conceptual comprehension demonstrated in guided settings.
2. `DEVELOPING`: Formative application with ongoing scaffolding and practice.
3. `DEMONSTRATED`: Empirical task execution with multi-source evidence.
4. `VALIDATED`: Formally verified by an authorized institutional validator.
5. `APPLIED`: Demonstrated in unscripted, real-world, or interdisciplinary settings.
6. `ADVANCED`: Deep synthesis, novel problem-solving, and peer mentorship.
7. `EXPIRED / REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`: Evidence decayed over time requiring contemporary demonstration.

## 4. Capability Mobility & Translation Contracts

Cross-institution evidence movement operates via governed cryptographic contracts:
- `CapabilityMobilityRequest`: Declares subject, source, destination, requested capabilities, evidence scopes, purpose, and learner consent token.
- `CapabilityMobilityResult`: Itemizes accepted, pending, and rejected evidence, mapping version, consent verification, and audit event ID.
- Receiving institutions retain full authority to review, accept, or reject evidence based on published departmental policies.
