## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Intent

Milestone N23 marks the transition of YOUVA-EdAI from connecting people to opportunities (N22) into a **Global Human Capability Intelligence & Learning Mobility Infrastructure**.

Building upon the foundations of N18 (Learning Science), N19 (Skills & Credentials), N20 (Lifelong Learning OS & Capability Graph), N21 (Ecosystem Intelligence), and N22 (Capability Exchange), N23 enables capability mobility, collective learning, knowledge contribution, lifelong participation, and human-to-human capability networks under the sovereign invariant:
> **YOUVA may help humans understand, develop, demonstrate, transfer and apply capability. It must never determine human worth, destiny, employability, social value, or the "optimal" life of an individual.**

$$\text{LEARN} \to \text{PRACTICE} \to \text{DEMONSTRATE} \to \text{VALIDATE} \to \text{BUILD CAPABILITY} \to \text{SHARE} \to \text{TEACH/MENTOR} \to \text{CONTRIBUTE} \to \text{COLLABORATE} \to \text{APPLY} \to \text{CREATE EXPERIENCE} \to \text{GENERATE NEW EVIDENCE} \to \text{REFLECT} \to \text{RELEARN}$$

## 2. Portable Capability Identity

Per **Clauses N23.10–N23.12**:
- The **Portable Human Capability Identity** stores decentralized pointers to capabilities, evidence, credentials, projects, and contributions—rather than a centralized surveillance profile.
- Strict identity separation is preserved:
  $$\mathbf{\text{Human Identity} \ne \text{Learning Record} \ne \text{Evidence} \ne \text{Credential} \ne \text{Capability} \ne \text{Opportunity History}}$$
- The learner maintains sovereign control over with whom, for what purpose, and for how long data is shared.

---

## 3. Capability Translation Framework

Per **Clauses N23.15–N23.18**:
- Institutions often use disparate terminology for overlapping capabilities.
- The **Capability Translation Layer** maps:
  $$\text{Institution A Terminology} \longrightarrow \text{YOUVA Common Capability Ontology} \longrightarrow \text{Institution B Terminology}$$
- **Invariant N23.16 (No False Equivalence)**: Two skills with similar names are not automatically equivalent. Mappings require explicit scope, proficiency level, evidence requirements, statistical confidence, and human review status.

---

## 4. Evidence Mobility

Per **Clause N23.13–N23.14**:
- Evidence mobility is executed via cryptographic contracts (`CapabilityMobilityRequest` $\to$ `CapabilityMobilityResult`).
- Requests require subject ID, source, destination, requested capabilities, evidence scopes, purpose limitation, and verified learner consent.
- Results partition evidence into `acceptedEvidence`, `pendingEvidence`, and `rejectedEvidence`, with an immutable audit event trail.

---

## 5. Cross-Institution Recognition

Per **Clauses N23.58–N23.64**:
- **Invariant N23.59**: Recognition remains the sovereign responsibility of the destination institution. YOUVA provides verifiable evidence; it cannot force recognition or rewrite original institutional records.
- If evidence is accepted, original credentials and destination interpretations remain separately recorded without silent conversion.

---

## 6. Capability Transfer

Per **Clauses N23.19–N23.20**:
- Transfer across domains (e.g. academic data science $\to$ industrial decision support) must be demonstrated, not assumed.
- The `TransferTaskEvaluation` framework evaluates learners on empirical transfer tasks; scores $\ge 70$ validate transfer.

---

## 7. Collective Learning

Per **Clauses N23.21–N23.23**:
- Moves beyond "What is the average learner?" to "How can groups learn more effectively together?"
- The Collective Learning Graph connects learners, educators, mentors, and institutions in purpose-bound, privacy-preserving relationships.

---

## 8. Peer Learning

Per **Clauses N23.24–N23.26**:
- Facilitates study groups, project teams, knowledge sharing, and peer review.
- Matching is driven by capability complementarity and learner preferences—**zero social ranking** or "most valuable student" scores.

---

## 9. Mentor Network

Per **Clauses N23.34–N23.39**:
- Expands mentorship matching based on experience, goals, availability, language, and context (no hidden personality profiling).
- Mandatory safety controls: role verification, communication controls, reporting, moderation, and minor protections.
- Prohibits dependency optimization or mentor attachment.

---

## 10. Contribution Network

Per **Clauses N23.27–N23.30**:
- Tracks contributions (mentoring, peer feedback, project code, OER, research summaries, community work).
- **Contribution $\ne$ Popularity**: Contributions are evaluated by peer/educator review and project outcomes, not likes, views, or follower counts.

---

## 11. Community Learning

Per **Clauses N23.101–N23.103**:
- Governed community spaces across coding, science, mathematics, arts, and languages.
- Community loops: $\text{Discuss} \to \text{Build} \to \text{Review} \to \text{Improve} \to \text{Validate} \to \text{Teach}$.

---

## 12. Knowledge Network

Per **Clauses N23.40–N23.44**:
- The Global Learning Knowledge Network models skills, capabilities, evidence, research, courses, projects, and artifacts.
- Provenance tracking: author, version, date, validation status, and evidence links.

---

## 13. Knowledge Provenance & Confidence States

Knowledge objects maintain explicit confidence states:
- `SUPPORTED`: Replicated empirical evidence.
- `PROBABLE`: Strong evidence pending independent replication.
- `UNCERTAIN`: Emerging hypothesis or limited sample.
- `DISPUTED`: Contradictory findings actively represented.
- `OUTDATED`: Superseded by contemporary research.

---

## 14. Evidence-Responsive Curriculum

Per **Clauses N23.47–N23.50**:
- Curricula adapt dynamically based on technology, societal needs, and learner outcomes.
- **Invariant N23.48**: AI can recommend updates; human curriculum authorities must approve consequential changes.
- Historical cohorts remain linked to their exact curriculum version.

---

## 15. Research Translation

Per **Clauses N23.45–N23.46**:
- Research does not automatically become teaching material.
- It passes through age-appropriateness checks, curriculum relevance, safety reviews, and SME validation.

---

## 16. Global Capability Intelligence

Per **Clauses N23.51–N23.57**:
- Aggregate demand signals synthesized from curriculum trends, research publications, and institutional data.
- Every trend quantifies sample size, time period, geographic scope, and statistical confidence.
- Prohibits speculative claims like *"AI will eliminate skill X"*; uses *"Evidence indicates increasing/decreasing demand signals"*.

---

## 17. Localization

Per **Clauses N23.56–N23.57**:
- Capability frameworks respect local language, culture, educational structure, and regulatory environments.
- Avoids cultural homogenization or assuming a single global reality.

---

## 18. Cross-Border Learning

Per **Clauses N23.58–N23.64**:
- Supports international mobility of validated capability evidence.
- Maintains destination institutional sovereignty over credential recognition.

---

## 19. Learning Wallet

Per **Clauses N23.65–N23.68**:
- The sovereign `LearningWalletReference` belongs to the learner.
- Contains references to capabilities, evidence, credentials, projects, and contributions.
- Guaranteed export in open, machine-readable formats (JSON-LD, W3C VC).

---

## 20. Interoperability

Per **Clauses N23.69–N23.70**:
- Aligns with W3C Verifiable Credentials, Open Badges 3.0, and IEEE Learning Technology standards.
- Interoperability registry maintains mapping versions, adapters, and deprecation schedules.

---

## 21. Identity Governance

Per **Clauses N23.85–N23.89**:
- Prohibits probabilistic identity guessing or automatic account merging.
- Identity conflicts trigger explicit human verification.

---

## 22. Privacy

Per **Clauses N23.79–N23.81**:
- Data minimization, pseudonymization, and small-cohort suppression ($n \ge 10$).
- Prohibits cross-domain telemetry combining learning with financial, biometric, or social data.

---

## 23. Consent

Per **Clauses N23.75–N23.78**:
- Scope-bound, recipient-bound, time-bound, revocable, and auditable consent.
- Research consent remains strictly decoupled from ordinary product usage; zero consent bundling.

---

## 24. Safety

Per **Clauses N23.185–N23.189**:
- Safety event architecture: $\text{Event} \to \text{Classification} \to \text{Escalation} \to \text{Human Review} \to \text{Action} \to \text{Audit}$.
- AI never acts as the final safety authority.

---

## 25. AI Governance

Per **Clauses N23.190–N23.200**:
- AI assists, organizes, translates, and summarizes.
- AI cannot independently assign permanent potential, make employment decisions, revoke identity, invalidate evidence, or issue credentials.

---

## 26. Human Agency

Per **Clauses N23.154 & N23.247**:
- System optimizes for **Human Capability + Human Agency**, never algorithmic optimization of humans.
- Learner retains freedom to choose, explore, challenge, or ignore recommendations.

---

## 27. AI Dependency Evaluation

Per **Clauses N23.155–N23.159**:
- **AI Removal Test**: Verifies whether learners can execute tasks when AI assistance is removed.
- Scaffold reduction and metacognitive reflection are triggered without creating punitive permanent "AI dependency" scores.

---

## 28. Fairness

Per **Clauses N23.201 & N23.210**:
- Monitored for false exclusion, capability misclassification, and mobility inequality.
- Neutral capability translation ensures non-discriminatory access.

---

## 29. Longitudinal Outcomes

Per **Clauses N23.254–N23.255**:
- Tracks capability retention, growth, and decay across multi-year horizons without causal overclaiming ($\text{Correlation} \ne \text{Causality}$).

---

## 30. Evidence Ledger

  $$\text{TESTED} \to \text{OBSERVED} \to \text{VALIDATED} \to \text{REPLICATED} \to \text{INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED} \to \text{REVALIDATED}$$
