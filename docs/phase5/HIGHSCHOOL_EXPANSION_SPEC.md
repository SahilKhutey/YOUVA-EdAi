# YOUVA-EdAi: Phase 5 High School Expansion Specification

## 1. Scope & Pedagogical Objectives
Phase 5 expands YOUVA-EdAi into the Secondary School tier (CBSE Class 10 Mathematics, ages 14–16), addressing the foundational curriculum unit:
**Class 10 Chapter 4 — Quadratic Equations (`MATH-G10-QUAD-01`)**.

---

## 2. Adolescent Pedagogical UX Directives
Secondary school students exhibit distinct cognitive and affective characteristics compared to middle school and primary students.

### 2.1 Anti-Gamification Principles
- **No Cartoon Avatars**: Replaced with clean, professional pseudonymized user identifiers (`did:youva:student:<hash>`).
- **No Extrinsic Token/Coin Economies**: Arbitrary points and coin economies are prohibited; replaced by empirical BKT mastery percentages.
- **No Celebration Confetti/Sound Effects**: Juvenile animation flourishes are removed to preserve cognitive focus and respect adolescent maturity.

### 2.2 Analytical Telemetry
- **Learning Velocity ($\Delta P(L) / \text{hr}$)**: Quantifies the student's rate of concept acquisition per hour of deliberate practice.
- **Concept Readiness Radar**: Visualizes prerequisite readiness across all 6 modular concept nodes of Quadratic Equations.
- **Learner Agency & Diagnostic Review**: Secondary students can self-select practice goals and trigger non-punitive diagnostic evaluations on demand.

---

## 3. Curriculum Structure: CBSE Grade 10 Quadratic Equations
