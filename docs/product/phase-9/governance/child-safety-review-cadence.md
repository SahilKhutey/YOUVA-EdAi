# YOUVA EdAI — Phase 9: Child Safety & Psychological Safeguarding Cadence (C12)
## Recurring Child Safety Audits, Distress Escalation Health Checks, and Incident Reviews

---

## 1. Governance Imperative

In educational software for minors, child safeguarding is the non-negotiable operational foundation:
> **Child safety reviews must be proactive, continuous, and independent of commercial product release timelines.**

---

## 2. Review Cadence & Protocol Architecture

| Cadence Dimension | Frequency / Trigger | Evaluated Safeguard & Scope | Mandatory Audit Owner | Action on Finding / Deficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Child Distress SLA Review** | **Bi-Monthly (60 Days)** | Verify that 100% of distress alerts escalated to human CSOs within \(\le 4\) hours. | Chief Child Safety Officer | Immediate alert queue restructuring; operational remediation |
| **Early Learner Safety Audit** | **Quarterly (90 Days)** | Review speech-to-text, avatar expressions, and audio cues for ages 5–9 (Phase 6 tier). | Developmental Psychologist | Voice/visual model parameter adjustment |
| **New Child-Facing Feature** | **Event-Based Trigger** | Mandatory pre-launch review of any interactive interface, gamification element, or hint prompt. | Child Safety Committee | Unilateral veto authority over launch |
| **Safety Incident Spike** | **Event-Based Trigger** | Any 24-hour period recording \(> 3\) student distress alerts triggers immediate crisis review. | Emergency Safeguarding Team | Deployment of localized human counselor intervention |
| **Statutory Age Law Update** | **Event-Based Trigger** | Any amendment to DPDP Act 2023 rules or COPPA revisions automatically triggers policy review. | General Counsel & CSO | Updated threshold in `jurisdiction.schema.json` |

---

## 3. Independent Child Psychology Panel

Annually, YOUVA EdAI convenes an external advisory panel consisting of certified educational psychologists, pediatric mental health professionals, and child rights advocates:
1. Audits anonymized student interaction samples for subtle cognitive distress or unhealthy attachment.
2. Validates that AI scaffolding promotes academic resilience rather than learned helplessness.
3. Issues a formal Public Child Safeguarding Report published in the Compliance Data Room.
