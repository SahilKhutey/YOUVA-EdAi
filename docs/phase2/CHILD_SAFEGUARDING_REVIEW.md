## 1. Executive Summary

This safeguarding review establishes the operational and algorithmic safety protocols governing children using the YOUVA-EdAI platform. Middle school learners (Ages 11–14, Grades 6–8) require proactive safeguarding boundaries, mental health distress detection, and strict human pastoral oversight.

---

## 2. Safeguarding Policy Commitments

1. **Zero Unfiltered Generative AI in the Child's Interaction Path**:
   - All AI interactions are constrained to pedagogical explanations and hints.
   - Generative hallucination or off-topic conversational drifting is intercepted.
2. **Proactive Trigger Interception**:
   - Continuous scanning for indicators of self-harm, suicidal ideation, child abuse, cyberbullying, or sexual harassment.
   - Triggers classified as `CRITICAL` or `HIGH` immediately notify human pastoral staff through out-of-band dual channels (SMS and Email).
3. **The "AI Cannot Close Alone" Non-Negotiable**:
   - In accordance with child welfare principles, an algorithm may never decide that a child is "safe" after an incident has been flagged.
   - Only a qualified human counselor, teacher, or safeguarding officer may resolve a safety incident following a direct welfare check.
4. **Data Minimization & Right to Erasure**:
   - Parents retain absolute authority to withdraw consent at any time.
   - Consent withdrawal immediately terminates the child's active session and triggers a guaranteed 24-hour cryptographic purge of personal identifiers.
