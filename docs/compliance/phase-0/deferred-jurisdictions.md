# YOUVA EdAI — Phase 0 Deferred Jurisdictions

## Explicitly Deferred Jurisdictions & Rationale

To maintain single-jurisdiction legal rigor and avoid multi-regime compliance failure at MVP stage, the following jurisdictions are **explicitly deferred**:

---

### 1. United States
- **Statutory Frameworks:** Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), State Student Privacy Laws (California SOPIPA, New York Ed Law 2-D).
- **Why Deferred:** Requires state-by-state student data privacy agreements (DPA/NDPA), complex district-level procurement reviews, and specialized FTC-compliant verifiable consent flows.
- **Scheduled Phase:** Phase 16 (Institutional & Market Scale).

---

### 2. European Union
- **Statutory Frameworks:** General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K Article 8), EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Annex III High-Risk Education Classification).
- **Why Deferred:** Variable member state age of digital consent thresholds (ranging from 13 to 16), complex cross-border Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), and extensive High-Risk AI conformity assessments.
- **Scheduled Phase:** Phase 16 (Institutional & Market Scale).

---

### 3. United Kingdom
- **Statutory Frameworks:** UK GDPR, Age-Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).
- **Why Deferred:** Comprehensive Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) and UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) child safety conformity requirements.
- **Scheduled Phase:** Phase 16 (Institutional & Market Scale).
