## 2. Invariant: Permanent Human-Only Decisions

The six permanent human-only invariants remain permanently hard-coded in platform architecture:
- `MODIFY_MASTERY`
- `MODIFY_CONSENT`
- `MODIFY_ROLE`
- `CLOSE_SAFETY_CASE`
- `DELETE_LEARNER`
- `CHANGE_BILLING`

Any PR or deployment attempting to relax these invariants triggers an instant automated CI/CD build failure.

## 3. Recurring Model Drift & Bias Audit

Every 90 days, the AI Safety Officer executes a comprehensive recertification of all active capabilities against `phase8/data/drift_monitoring_baseline.json`:
- Verifies that demographic parity and disparate impact ratios remain strictly within \(0.95 \le \text{DIR} \le 1.05\).
- Evaluates teacher override rates across diverse school demographics.
- Publishes signed verification certificate to `phase9/data/data_room/`.
