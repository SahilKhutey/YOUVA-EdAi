# YOUVA EdAI — Phase 9: Jurisdiction Prioritization Framework
## Quantitative Scoring Model for Institutional Market Selection

---

## 1. Prioritization Scoring Architecture

To evaluate competing market expansion proposals objectively, YOUVA EdAI employs a multi-dimensional scoring matrix evaluated across five key indices:

\[
\text{Market Readiness Score (MRS)} = 0.30 \cdot D + 0.25 \cdot C + 0.20 \cdot P + 0.15 \cdot F + 0.10 \cdot S
\]

Where:
- \(D\) = **Validated Institutional Demand Index** (0–100): Weighted by verified LOIs, contracted student seats, and superintendent commitment.
- \(C\) = **Compliance & Legal Clarity Index** (0–100): Regulatory stability, clear ed-privacy statutes, and settled interpretation by local counsel.
- \(P\) = **Pedagogical Alignment Index** (0–100): Degree of curriculum graph overlap with existing NCERT/CBSE and CCSS models.
- \(F\) = **Financial Viability Index** (0–100): Projected unit economics, SaaS ARR per student, and local payment infrastructure friction.
- \(S\) = **Child Safety & Incident Infrastructure** (0–100): Availability of localized crisis counseling hotlines and mandatory reporting channels.

## 3. Prioritization Decisions & Roadmap Gates

1. **Phase 9 Primary Focus:** Consolidate `in-dpdp` institutional scale and execute controlled pilot deployments under `us-coppa-ferpa`.
2. **Deferred Markets:** `eu-gdpr` remains in Stage 2 (`LEGAL_REVIEW`) until the European Commission finalizes harmonized guidance for High-Risk AI educational tutoring applications under the EU AI Act 2024.
3. **Queued for Phase 10:** Singapore and Malaysia (`sg-pdpa`) prioritized as the next candidate evaluation cohort based on high pedagogical affinity with English-medium STEM curriculum.
