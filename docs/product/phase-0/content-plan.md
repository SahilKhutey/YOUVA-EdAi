# YOUVA EdAI — Phase 0 Content Production Plan

## Scope
- **Grade:** Middle School (Grade 7 or 8)
- **Subject:** Mathematics
- **Curriculum Standard:** NCERT / CBSE
- **Locked Units:** Linear Equations in One Variable & Rational Numbers / Fractions

## Sourcing Strategy
**HYBRID APPROACH:**
1. Base mathematical problem stems adapted from open NCERT / OER curricular frameworks.
2. Step decomposition, misconception triggers, and 3-tier Socratic progressive hints authored in-house by an accredited mathematics educator.

## Content Types
- Diagnostic items (5 items calibrating $P(L_0)$)
- Practice items (35 multi-step items)
- Worked examples (5 annotated demonstrations)
- Socratic Hints (3 tiers per item: Conceptual $\to$ Procedural $\to$ Bottom-out)
- Remediation items (5 prerequisite refresher items)

## Content QA Workflow
```
Author (Math Educator)
      ↓
Subject Expert Review (Independent Teacher)
      ↓
Curriculum Alignment & Misconception Verification
      ↓
Difficulty Calibration & Psychometric Indexing
      ↓
Answer & Step Verification (Double-Blind Solving)
      ↓
AI Safety & Edge-Case Prompt Review
      ↓
Seeding & Production Deployment
```
