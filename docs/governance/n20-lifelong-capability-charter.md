## 1. Constitutional Preamble & Strategic Progression

Milestone N19 established the YOUVA Skills & Credential Network:
$$\text{Learning} \longrightarrow \text{Assessment} \longrightarrow \text{Evidence} \longrightarrow \text{Skill} \longrightarrow \text{Credential} \longrightarrow \text{Portable Verification}$$

Milestone N20 transforms YOUVA into a **Lifelong Human Capability Operating System**:
$$\text{Learning} \longrightarrow \text{Capability} \longrightarrow \text{Opportunity} \longrightarrow \text{Application} \longrightarrow \text{Outcome} \longrightarrow \text{Continuous Reskilling}$$

The platform evolves from an episodic course/credential system into a lifelong capability navigation platform that empowers the learner throughout their educational, professional, and personal journey.

## 2. Foundational Non-Negotiable Invariants

### Invariant 1: The Non-Equivalence Doctrine (Clause N20.3, N20.116)
$$\mathbf{\text{Prediction} \ne \text{Capability} \ne \text{Credential} \ne \text{Course Completion} \ne \text{AI Recommendation} \ne \text{Interest} \ne \text{Potential}}$$

1. **Prediction is not Capability**: An AI model predicting that a learner "can probably write a compiler" does not establish capability. Capability requires demonstrated, authentic evidence under authentic constraints.
2. **Capability is not Credential**: A learner may hold a capability without a formal institutional credential; conversely, a credential may have decayed or lost empirical validity.
3. **Course Completion is not Capability**: Passive attendance, video watch time, or multiple-choice completion is never recorded as demonstrated capability.
4. **No Single Scalar Potential Score (Clause N20.68, N20.119)**: Capability must NEVER be compressed into a single scalar "human potential score", IQ proxy, or employability rating. Human potential is multidimensional, context-dependent, and evolving.

### Invariant 2: Capability $\ne$ Skill (Clause N20.5)
- A **Skill** is an isolated unit of competence (e.g., recursion, SQL joins, sterile pipetting).
- A **Capability** integrates skills, problem-solving, context sensitivity, independence, transferability, and execution in authentic scenarios (e.g., designing, deploying, and maintaining a secure data ingestion service).

### Invariant 3: Human-in-the-Loop & Learner Agency (Clauses N20.22, N20.179–N20.180, N20.204)
$$\text{Governance Triangle: AI (Intelligence) } \longleftrightarrow \text{ Human (Governance) } \longleftrightarrow \text{ Learner (Agency)}$$
1. **No Consequential AI Autonomy**: The AI Capability Coach and Opportunity Intelligence Engine are strictly **advisory and navigational**. AI must NEVER autonomously hire, reject, admit, deny credentials, or determine life trajectories.
2. **Learner Agency**: The learner retains ultimate sovereignty over their goals, pathways, shared evidence, and visibility permissions.
3. **Teacher / Mentor Override**: Authorized educators and mentors retain unconditional override authority over AI recommendations with auditable reason codes.

### Invariant 4: Separation of Distinct Graphs (Clause N20.14)
The system strictly maintains separation of ontological spaces:
$$\text{Skills Graph} \ne \text{Evidence Graph} \ne \text{Credential Graph} \ne \text{Capability Graph} \ne \text{Opportunity Graph} \ne \text{Outcome Graph}$$
No single undifferentiated graph may collapse capability into evidence or opportunity into credential.

### Invariant 5: Opportunity Matching $\ne$ Selection (Clauses N20.27–N20.34)
Matching an opportunity (internship, project, apprenticeship, job, research) is an exploratory compatibility analysis. It is never an automated employment or selection decision. External opportunity ingestion must sanitize inputs against prompt and content injection attacks.

### Invariant 6: Outcome Correlation $\ne$ Causality (Clause N20.46)
Longitudinal tracking of outcomes (employment, wage progression, research output, career resilience) records observed empirical correlations. It is constitutionally forbidden to market or report these correlations as proven causal proof of platform efficacy without pre-registered randomized control or rigorous counterfactual validation.

## 3. Human Capability Dimensions & Progression Levels

Capabilities are evaluated across five orthogonal dimensions:
1. **Application**: Ability to apply the knowledge in authentic, unstructured settings.
2. **Independence**: Execution without scaffolding or automated assistance.
3. **Transfer**: Ability to generalize capability across novel domains and tooling.
4. **Recency**: Timestamp of recent authentic demonstration.
5. **Evidence Strength**: Cryptographic provenance and multi-source verification score.

Progression Levels:
$$\text{FOUNDATION} \longrightarrow \text{DEVELOPING} \longrightarrow \text{INDEPENDENT} \longrightarrow \text{ADVANCED} \longrightarrow \text{EXPERT}$$

Capabilities do not automatically expire or disappear; when evidence becomes stale, the capability state transitions to `NEEDS_EVIDENCE` or `STALE`, prompting low-friction revalidation rather than punitive revocation.

## 4. AI Removal Test & Cognitive Independence (Clause N20.138)

To ensure that the learner does not become cognitively dependent on the AI Capability Coach, the system periodically applies the **AI Removal Test**:
- If a learner's capability performance drops by $> 35\%$ when AI scaffolding is removed, the system issues a `CAPABILITY_AI_DEPENDENCY_FLAG`.
- The pathway automatically injects unassisted problem-solving exercises to rebuild autonomous cognitive agency.

## 5. Security, Privacy & Adversarial Protections

1. **Prompt & Content Injection Defense (Clause N20.186)**: All external opportunity listings, employer descriptions, and portfolio imports are scrubbed through strict adversarial filtering before entering the Opportunity Graph.
2. **Zero Leaderboards of Human Worth (Clause N20.119)**: Capability profiles are private to the learner and selectively disclosed via verifiable cryptographic presentations. Public leaderboards ranking human worth are strictly prohibited.
3. **Anti-Inflation Audit**: Capability inflation signals are continuously monitored to prevent credential creep, synthetic evidence generation, and collusion.
