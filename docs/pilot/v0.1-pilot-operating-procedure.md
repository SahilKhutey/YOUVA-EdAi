# YOUVA EdAI v0.1 — Pilot Operating Procedure & Session Protocol

> **Document ID:** PILOT-SOP-001  
> **Status:** RATIFIED & ACTIVE  
> **Target Scope:** YOUVA EdAI v0.1 Minimum Viable Learning Loop  
> **Subject:** Middle School Math (Grade 6–8)  
> **Single Concept:** One-Step Linear Equations (`one-step-equations`)

---

## 1. Scope Boundary Enforcement

YOUVA EdAI v0.1 is intentionally restricted to the smallest real learning loop between a real student, adaptive difficulty, and a supervising teacher.

| Hard Included Scope | Explicitly Excluded (Out of Scope for v0.1) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Middle School (Grade 6–8)** | Pre-School, Elementary, High School, Higher-Ed |
| **Math: One-Step Linear Equations** | Multi-step equations, geometry, science, other subjects |
| **18 Curated Text Questions (6 Easy, 6 Med, 6 Hard)** | Image, video, audio generation, speech synthesis |
| **Deterministic Adaptive Rule (`nextDifficulty`)** | Machine learning models, BKT parameter fitting |
| **Clean, Distraction-Free Student Screen** | Gamification, badges, avatars, leaderboards, chat |
| **Single-Table Teacher Oversight View** | Full institutional analytics, district reporting |
| **One Teacher Override (`[ NEEDS HELP ]`)** | Autonomous AI agents, automatic policy engines |
| **Documented In-Person Guardian Consent** | Full automated legal consent platforms |
| **Adult Supervision & Physical Presence** | Unsupervised remote consumer app usage |

## 2. Minimum Safety Operating Procedure (Step 9 Protocol)

Because this pilot involves a real child, the operational safety layer is provided by a documented manual protocol and in-person adult presence:

### Before the Session:
1. **Verifiable Guardian Consent:** The pilot facilitator or teacher directly obtains and verifies signed/documented parental consent from the student's legal guardian.
2. **Contact Verification:** The guardian's phone number or email is confirmed and recorded in the pilot registry.
3. **Physical Supervision:** A parent, guardian, or qualified educator is confirmed to be personally present in the room or immediately reachable.
4. **Student Onboarding:** The student is introduced to the interface. The facilitator clarifies that:
   - This is short math practice.
   - It is not an exam or grading test.
   - They can pause or stop at any time.
5. **Data Minimization (DPDP Standard):** Only the student's first name / pseudonym and mathematical attempt responses are stored. No biometric data, location tracking, camera/mic access, or device identifiers are collected.

### During the Session:
1. **Continuous Availability:** The supervising adult remains attentive.
2. **Behavioral Observation:** If the student shows signs of acute frustration or confusion, the teacher uses the **`[ NEEDS HELP ]`** override button to deliver a foundational review question or steps in personally.
3. **Emergency Stop:** If any system error, confusion, or distress occurs, the session is halted immediately.

### After the Session:
1. **Immediate Session Review:** Teacher and facilitator review the student's attempt sequence (Questions $\rightarrow$ Responses $\rightarrow$ Adaptive Transitions).
2. **Log Verification:** Confirm that the override executed correctly and modified subsequent item selection.
3. **Data Hygiene:** Any scratch notes containing personal contact details are safely archived; only anonymous educational traces are retained for analysis.
4. **Completion Sign-Off:** Facilitator and teacher fill out the **v0.1 Completion Record** below.
