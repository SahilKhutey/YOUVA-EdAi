## 4. The 25 Articles of the YOUVA-EdAI Constitution (Clause N∞.66)

1. **Humans Remain the Purpose**: Technology must always remain subordinate to human development and educational flourishing.
2. **Learning Over Engagement**: The system optimizes for retention, transfer, and independent capability, never screen time, streaks, or click volume.
3. **Evidence-Grounded Capability**: Capability must be demonstrated through empirical evidence, never inferred through speculative AI scores.
4. **Provenance & Uncertainty**: Every piece of evidence and knowledge object retains author, version, date, and calibrated uncertainty.
5. **Authorized Credentials**: Credentials must represent verified, authorized evidence issued by recognized evaluating authorities.
6. **AI Capability Does Not Create AI Authority**: The advancement of an AI model's fluency does not grant it autonomous decision-making power over humans.
7. **Human Consequential Decision Authority**: Consequential decisions (admissions, hiring, certification, sanctions) require external human or governed authorization.
8. **Enhanced Child Protection**: Safety, parental presence, and developmental appropriateness strictly override personalization, engagement, and autonomy for minor learners.
9. **Privacy by Design**: Data minimization, tenant isolation, and small-cohort protection ($n \ge 10$) are hard requirements, not afterthoughts.
10. **Sovereign Learner Agency**: Learners retain meaningful ownership of their data with the right to inspect, export, correct, revoke, or challenge claims.
11. **Recommendations Are Not Destiny**: Algorithmic suggestions are exploratory options; they never define what a learner should become.
12. **Prediction Is Not Truth**: Statistical probability models can never substitute for empirical evidence of competence.
13. **Prohibition of Human Potential Ranking**: Assigning permanent scalar "potential scores", "employability indexes", or innate talent rankings is constitutionally barred.
14. **Institutions Do Not Own Universal Truth**: Institutional records reflect localized context; they do not hold universal hegemony over a human's capability.
15. **Employers Do Not Own Learning Truth**: Commercial organizations evaluate candidates for specific roles; they do not determine human educational worth.
16. **AI Does Not Own Educational Truth**: Generative models are assistive tools, not the authoritative arbiter of knowledge or human capability.
17. **Right to Challenge & Correct**: Learners and educators maintain governed appeal workflows to contest evidence mappings or algorithmic assessments.
18. **Empirical Claim Justification**: Product and educational claims must progress through the 7-level evidence hierarchy before publication.
19. **Mandatory Negative Evidence Retention**: Failed pedagogical interventions, cognitive overload incidents, and model errors are permanently recorded.
20. **Continuous Independent Oversight**: Safety, privacy, security, and educational outcomes undergo regular third-party audits.
21. **Demand-Gated Scale**: System scaling is gated by verified educational evidence, safety capacity, and operational resilience.
22. **Continuous Security & Safety**: Vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, red teaming, and disaster recovery drills are continuous obligations.
23. **Interoperability Without Lock-In**: The system adheres to open standards (W3C VC, Open Badges 3.0, IEEE LTSC); learners never lose evidence by leaving YOUVA.
24. **Subordination of Technology**: AI serves as bounded infrastructure (Observe, Explain, Generate, Translate, Recommend, Assist), never as an unconstrained ruler.
25. **Continuous Self-Evaluation**: YOUVA must continuously verify whether YOUVA itself is actually helping humans learn and thrive.
