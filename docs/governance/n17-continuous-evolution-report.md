## 1. Executive Summary & Post-Roadmap Strategic Overview

Milestone N17 marks the first post-roadmap evolution cycle in the history of YOUVA-EdAI. With the full 16-phase buildout (`Build → Validate → Pilot → Scale → Govern`) completed, N17 transitions the platform from a project into a permanent **Evolution Operating System (Evolution OS)**:

$$\begin{matrix}
\text{N1–N16: Project Development Roadmap} \\
\Downarrow \\
\text{N17+: Permanent Evolution OS} \\
\text{Learn} \longrightarrow \text{Research} \longrightarrow \text{Benchmark} \longrightarrow \text{Improve} \longrightarrow \text{Re-Verify}
\end{matrix}$$

N17 does not introduce arbitrary feature expansion; rather, it introduces the empirical and governance machinery that decides *what to build, what to research, what to stop building, and what to retire*.

## 2. Core Doctrine & Invariants (Clause N17.2)

Every system in N17 is bound by the eight non-negotiable governing axioms:
1. **$\text{Evidence} > \text{Assumption}$**: No educational claim or model promotion without empirical data.
2. **$\text{Learning Outcome} > \text{Engagement}$**: Optimization for true comprehension over screen-time.
3. **$\text{Human Agency} > \text{Automation}$**: AI recommends; human educators authorize.
4. **$\text{Safety} > \text{Growth}$**: Expansion halts if safety or child protection bounds are threatened.
5. **$\text{Privacy} > \text{Data Accumulation}$**: Minimization and strict purpose limitation.
6. **$\text{Educational Validity} > \text{AI Novelty}$**: Generative AI is deployed only where pedagogically superior.
7. **$\text{Reversibility} > \text{Irreversible Automation}$**: All decisions, states, and models support clean rollback.
8. **$\text{Independent Verification} > \text{Self-Certification}$**: AI models cannot evaluate or certify themselves.

### Foundational Invariants
- $\mathbf{\text{Capability} \ne \text{Authority}}$
- $\mathbf{\text{No Self-Expansion for Agents}}$
- $\mathbf{\text{No Commercial Data Monetization by Default}}$
