## 2. Institutional SLA Reporting Dashboard

On the 1st of each calendar month, an automated report is generated for institutional school heads:
- **Contract Reference**: `CONTRACT-DPSRKP-2026-SCALE`
- **Uptime Measured**: Calculated over school operational hours (07:30 to 18:30 IST).
- **Seat Utilization**: Peak concurrent active students vs. licensed quota.
- **Safety Incidents**: Zero-PII attestation of any safety triggers and resolution timestamps.
