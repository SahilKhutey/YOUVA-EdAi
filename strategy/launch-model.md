# YOUVA-EdAI — Strategy: Launch Business Model
## Canonical Strategy Document | Phase 0 Scope Lock

**Status:** RATIFIED & LOCKED  
**Selected Launch Model:** B2B School-First (Design Partner Classroom Pilot)  
**Deferred Model:** B2C Direct-to-Consumer Family Subscriptions  
**Date Locked:** 2026-09-25  

---

## 1. Decision: B2B School-First

YOUVA-EdAI will launch exclusively as a **B2B School-First platform**, beginning with a single-classroom design partnership.

---

## 2. Decision Framework: Why B2B Solves the Core Hypothesis

| Factor | B2B School-First (Selected) | B2C Family-First (Deferred) |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Authority Invariant** | **Built-in:** Real teacher is physically present, observing students and executing authoritative overrides daily. | **Missing:** Unsupervised child or busy parent; cannot test the "AI recommends, teacher authorizes" hypothesis. |
| **Attendance & Churn** | **Stable:** 100% daily classroom attendance; zero churn during instructional periods. | **Volatile:** High Day-14 drop-off; customer acquisition cost (CAC) drains early capital. |
| **Qualitative Feedback** | **Direct & Immediate:** Daily debriefs with a professional math educator catching subtle pedagogical flaws. | **Diffused:** Asynchronous customer support tickets; noisy survey responses. |
| **Billing Complexity** | **Zero at Launch:** Operating under a zero-cost Educational Research Charter; no payment gateway required. | **High:** Immediate dependency on Stripe checkout, dunning, card declines, and refund disputes. |

---

## 3. Pilot Partner Structure

- **Partner Institution:** Delhi Public School, Math Department (Class 7B).
- **Cohort Size:** 30 Students, 1 Lead Educator (Mrs. Meenakshi Sundaram), 1 Department Head Observer.
- **Agreement Instrument:** Mutual Educational Pilot Collaboration Agreement (research license, zero commercial fees, strict data minimization).
- **Expansion Milestone:** Moving beyond Class 7B to the full Grade 7 & 9 cohorts requires passing the Phase 3 / Phase 7 commercial demand gates.
