## 1. Selected Launch Tier: Middle School (Grades 6–8)

### Target Grade Band
**Grade 7 (Ages 11–13)** is locked as the exact starting cohort for the YOUVA-EdAI MVP.

### Why Middle School (Decision Framework & Rationale)
1. **Cognitive & Pedagogical Sweet Spot:** Middle school represents the critical developmental leap from concrete arithmetic to abstract symbolic reasoning (pre-algebra, integer operations, equations). It is the global inflection point where student math confidence either solidifies or cliffs.
2. **Interaction Fit:** Grade 7 students possess full reading comprehension and keyboard literacy. They can interact via structured Socratic dialogue and scratchpad equations without requiring the voice-first/non-reading UX needed for younger learners.
3. **Safety & Risk Envelope:** Unlike High School (where transcript GPA, board exam stakes, and college applications create immediate institutional and legal exposure), Middle School enables rigorous testing of the adaptive learning loop in a lower-stakes environment.
4. **Architectural Alignment:** Directly reuses the repository's 4-parameter Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) engine and teacher-override cockpit without ground-up frontend restructuring.

---

## 2. Explicitly Deferred Tiers

The following age tiers are strictly deferred and prohibited from MVP build sprints:

1. **Pre-School & Kindergarten (Ages 3–7):** Deferred to Phase 6b / Phase 13.1. Requires ground-up voice/tactile UI, zero-chat sandbox, and specialized early childhood developmental safety reviews.
2. **Elementary (Ages 7–11):** Deferred to Phase 6a / Phase 13.2. Requires visual block-based logic, homework co-pilot ergonomics, and guided parental controls.
3. **High School (Ages 15–18):** Deferred to Phase 5 / Phase 12. Requires board exam preparation suites, advanced calculus/kinematics, and W3C Skills Passport credentials.
4. **Higher Education & Adult Learning (Ages 18–24):** **Officially retired from core platform scope** as premature scope creep.

---

## 3. The Core Product Thesis Under Test

> *"Can an AI tutor and a human teacher collaborate so reliably that real students measurably learn and the teacher genuinely trusts and uses the system rather than routing around it?"*

If this question is not answered affirmatively with empirical evidence from Grade 7 classrooms, no subsequent tier will be built.
