## Sign-Off Requirements

A locked scope requires explicit written approval from four distinct stakeholders:

1. **Product Lead:** Confirms operational feasibility and adherence to the single-tier MVP scope.
2. **Founder / Vision Owner:** Confirms alignment with long-term strategy and authorizes resource expenditure.
3. **Pilot Partner (School / Educator):** Confirms institutional willingness, classroom access, and curriculum schedule alignment.
4. **Legal / Compliance Counsel:** Confirms the data processing model, consent mechanisms, and regulatory boundaries comply with applicable law.
