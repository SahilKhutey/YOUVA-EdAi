## 1. Launch Tier

**Selected tier:** Middle School  
**Grade:** Grade 7 / 8  
**Deferred Tiers:**
- Kindergarten & Junior
- High School

### Rationale
Middle school (ages 11–14) provides the optimal balance for MVP validation: students possess sufficient literacy for text-based cognitive engagement and mathematical problem solving without early-childhood voice/non-reader constraints, while avoiding the high-stakes transcript and graduation credential liability of high school.

## 5. Explicitly Out of Scope

- Billing & Stripe subscriptions

- Additional educational tiers (Early childhood / High school)

---

## 6. Product Thesis

YOUVA EdAI will validate whether an AI-assisted adaptive learning system and a human teacher can collaborate effectively enough to improve student learning while maintaining teacher trust and meaningful human control.
