# Audit Log: Deprecation and Purge of Speculative Cognitive Metrics

## 1. Governance Mandate
Under DPDP Act 2023 §9, psychometric validity standards, and ethical AI guidelines for minors, YOUVA-EdAi mandates the permanent exclusion of unfalsifiable, invasive, or pseudo-scientific cognitive profiling metrics.

---

## 2. Inventory of Purged Speculative Metrics

### 2.1 Cognitive Twin State (`CognitiveTwinState`)
- **Status**: **PURGED & FORBIDDEN**
- **Defect Rationale**: Presumes an internalized digital replica of a student's brain. In reality, such models are opaque hallucinations that lack psychometric grounding and violate data minimization principles.
- **Empirical Replacement**: Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) 4-parameter model ($P(L_0), P(T), P(G), P(S)$) producing mathematically auditable mastery probabilities.

### 2.2 Learning Style Categorization (`VARK_LearningStyleHeuristic`)
- **Status**: **PURGED & FORBIDDEN**
- **Defect Rationale**: Extensive cognitive psychology research (Pashler et al., 2008) has debunked the notion that categorizing learners into "Visual", "Auditory", or "Kinesthetic" improves educational outcomes. It pigeonholes students and narrows curriculum delivery.
- **Empirical Replacement**: Multi-tiered scaffolding (Conceptual $\to$ Strategic $\to$ Concrete) available dynamically to all learners based on prerequisite mastery.

### 2.3 Inferred Neuro-Attentional Index (`NeuroAttentionalIndex`)
- **Status**: **PURGED & FORBIDDEN**
- **Defect Rationale**: Guessing neural engagement from mouse movements or video frames is scientifically dubious, intrusive, and creates false inferences of ADHD or disengagement.
- **Empirical Replacement**: Transparent observable metrics: task response latency and step error rates.

### 2.4 Emotional Valence Guessing (`EmotionalValenceGuessing`)
- **Status**: **PURGED & FORBIDDEN**
- **Defect Rationale**: Computer-vision or text-sentiment emotion guessing is notoriously inaccurate across diverse cultural contexts and generates biased labels for minors.
- **Empirical Replacement**: Student-initiated help requests and teacher observations.

### 2.5 Static Aptitude / IQ Projections (`InnateAptitudeIQProjection`)
- **Status**: **PURGED & FORBIDDEN**
- **Defect Rationale**: Fixed-aptitude labeling violates pedagogical growth-mindset principles and risks systemic educational redlining.
- **Empirical Replacement**: Dynamic, continuous mastery progression modeling.
