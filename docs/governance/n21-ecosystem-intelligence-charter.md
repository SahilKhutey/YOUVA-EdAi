## 1. Constitutional Preamble & Strategic Transition

Milestones N1–N20 established:
- N1–N16: Governed, institution-grade AI Learning Operating System.
- N17: Permanent Continuous Evolution & Research OS.
- N18: Advanced Learning Science & Cognitive Personalization.
- N19: Global Skills, Evidence & Credential Network.
- N20: Lifelong Learning OS & Human Capability Graph.

Milestone N21 expands the platform into a **Global Learning & Human Capability Ecosystem Intelligence Layer**:
$$\text{Learner} \longleftrightarrow \text{Teacher} \longleftrightarrow \text{Institution} \longleftrightarrow \text{Credential Issuer} \longleftrightarrow \text{Mentor} \longleftrightarrow \text{Researcher} \longleftrightarrow \text{Opportunity Provider} \longleftrightarrow \text{Employer}$$

The system answers the fundamental question:  
*How can YOUVA understand and improve the health, effectiveness, interoperability, and evolution of an entire learning ecosystem without turning learners into surveillance subjects or allowing AI to make consequential life decisions?*

## 2. Foundational Non-Negotiable Invariants

### Invariant 1: The 7-Layer Truth Model (Clause N21.3)
$$\mathbf{\text{Learner Truth} \ne \text{Institution Truth} \ne \text{Evidence Truth} \ne \text{Credential Truth} \ne \text{Capability Truth} \ne \text{Opportunity Truth} \ne \text{Employment Truth}}$$
No single participant or algorithmic model may unilaterally collapse or overwrite these distinct layers.

### Invariant 2: Ecosystem Optimization Principle (Clause N21.2)
Optimize the learning ecosystem for **validated human capability and educational outcomes** — *not* for engagement, credential volume, institutional activity, commercial monetization, or AI autonomy.

### Invariant 3: Ecosystem Intelligence $\ne$ Surveillance (Clauses N21.12–N21.18, N21.145)
1. **Minimization & Purpose Limitation**: The ecosystem operates on decentralized federation, pseudonymous aggregation, and selective disclosure.
2. **Small-Cohort Protection (Clauses N21.17–N21.18)**: Aggregate statistics for any cohort smaller than the privacy threshold ($n < 10$) are strictly suppressed to prevent re-identification.
3. **No Hidden Cross-Domain Inference (Clause N21.145)**: The system strictly prohibits combining learning telemetry with financial, social, or biometric data to synthesize human capability scores.

### Invariant 4: No Universal Truth Graph & Explicit Uncertainty (Clauses N21.8–N21.9, N21.175)
The Ecosystem Graph does not assume omniscience. Every relationship edge must record:
- `authority`: Sponsoring organization or evaluation authority.
- `confidence`: Calibrated statistical confidence (0.0–1.0).
- `status`: One of `SUPPORTED`, `PROBABLE`, `CANDIDATE`, `DISPUTED`, `UNCERTAIN`, or `STALE`.
- `scope`: Jurisdictional and institutional boundary.

### Invariant 5: External Event Untrust & Authority Matrix (Clauses N21.27–N21.30, N21.41–N21.44)
1. External ecosystem events are **untrusted** by default.
2. External systems **cannot directly mutate authoritative learning mastery** without passing through signature verification, schema validation, tenant scoping, idempotency replay protection, and the formal Authority Matrix.
3. **No Silent Overwrites (Clause N21.44)**: Discrepancies between external records and YOUVA records transition to a governed `CONFLICT` state rather than being silently overwritten.

### Invariant 6: Anti-Ranking & Non-Consequential AI Determination (Clauses N21.64, N21.108–N21.109, N21.214)
1. **No Universal Human Ranking**: Generating generalized human worth scores or individual employability rankings is constitutionally barred.
2. **No Autonomous Consequential Decisions**: AI models may summarize, forecast, detect anomalies, and recommend. The learner chooses; human educators, institutions, and employers govern.

## 3. Partner Lifecycle & Scoped Authorization

External organizations receive an auditable `EcosystemPartner` identity:
$$\text{APPLIED} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFIED} \longrightarrow \text{ONBOARDED} \longrightarrow \text{ACTIVE} \longrightarrow \text{REVIEW} \longrightarrow \text{SUSPENDED / OFFBOARDED}$$

- **Strict Least Privilege (Clause N21.24)**: No wildcard scopes (`*`) are permitted. Valid scopes include `READ_SHARED_EVIDENCE`, `SUBMIT_EVIDENCE`, `VERIFY_CREDENTIAL`, `PUBLISH_OPPORTUNITY`, and `ACCESS_AGGREGATE_ANALYTICS`.
- **Offboarding Decoupling (Clause N21.129)**: Partner offboarding revokes API access immediately but **never invalidates legitimate learner-owned credentials or verified evidence**.

---

## 4. Federated Analytics & Privacy Architecture

1. **Differential Privacy & Aggregation**: Aggregate queries are evaluated with mathematical privacy guarantees.
2. **Role-Based Views**:
   - *Learners*: Sovereign view of personal goals, opportunities, and verified credentials.
   - *Teachers*: Cohort misconception patterns and curriculum effectiveness without punitive ranking.
   - *Institutions*: Structural capability gaps and program-level outcomes.
   - *Researchers*: Pre-registered trials and anonymized evidence ledgers.
