# YOUVA EdAI — Phase 6: Early Learner Pilot Operational Protocol
## Field Procedures, Observer Guidelines, and Child Distress Intervention Protocols

---

## 1. Onboarding & VPC Verification Protocol

1. **Step 1: In-Person Briefing**: Parents and students attend a 30-minute orientation. Observers demonstrate that the system contains zero ads, zero open chat, and auto-locks at 15 minutes.
2. **Step 2: VPC Authentication**: Parent provides mobile number; inputs 6-digit SMS OTP. The platform registers consent hash in the immutable ledger.
3. **Step 3: Device Pairing**: Parent scans QR code from their mobile companion app to pair with the child's tablet session.

---

## 2. In-Session Observation Protocol

Observers (psychologist, teacher, and researcher) maintain a discreet distance ($\ge 2\text{ meters}$) to observe natural behavior without inducing performance anxiety:

### Observation Checklist
- **Physical Demeanor**: Relaxed posture vs. physical tensing, furrowed brows, or eye rubbing.
- **Engagement Style**: Independent tapping vs. looking at parent for validation before every choice.
- **Vocal Reaction**: Cheerful humming, repeating spoken audio vs. sighs, frustration, or silence.
- **Motor Control**: Direct single taps vs. frantic repeated slapping or dragging.
- **Response to Mistakes**: Calmly trying again vs. withdrawal, tears, or pushing tablet away.

---

## 3. Immediate Distress & Intervention Triggers

If any of the following distress triggers occur, the observer or parent must immediately press the **Emergency Pause / Stop** button:

```
Distress Indicator Triggers (Immediate Pause Mandate)
├── Child sheds tears or vocalizes sadness ("I can't do this")
├── Child exhibits continuous avoidance behavior (looking away > 60 seconds)
├── Child asks repetitive anxious questions about losing points or failing
├── Audio asset glitches, producing harsh feedback, screeching, or loud pop
└── Device temperature rises noticeably or battery drops rapidly
```

### Post-Intervention Debrief
When an intervention occurs:
1. The session is terminated with zero academic record.
2. The child is immediately engaged in an offline physical activity (drawing, wooden blocks).
3. The observer logs an incident ticket in the Child Safety Review register within 30 minutes.
