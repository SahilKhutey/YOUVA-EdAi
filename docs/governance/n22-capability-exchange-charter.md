## 2. Foundational Constitutional Invariants

### Invariant 1: The 9-Layer Constitutional Separation (Clause N22.198)
$$\mathbf{\text{Learning Truth} \ne \text{Evidence Truth} \ne \text{Credential Truth} \ne \text{Employment Decision} \ne \text{Recommendation} \ne \text{Prediction} \ne \text{Experience} \ne \text{Capability} \ne \text{Human Worth}}$$
No algorithmic model, institutional partner, corporate employer, or platform heuristic may collapse, cross-multiply, or synthesize these distinct layers into a single scalar ranking.

### Invariant 2: Evidence Over Prediction Principle (Clause N22.4)
$$\mathbf{\text{Capability Evidence} > \text{AI Prediction}} \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{\text{Validated Evidence} > \text{Self-Reported Claims} > \text{AI-Inferred Claims}}$$
Matching and opportunity recommendations must strictly be grounded in auditable, validated evidence of demonstrated capability. Statistical probability models or speculative AI predictions can never substitute for empirical evidence of competence.

### Invariant 3: Capability-to-Opportunity Boundary (Clauses N22.5, N22.13, N22.41)
- **Strictly Permitted**: Transparent, explainable capability alignment:
  *"Your demonstrated skills overlap with these opportunity requirements: [A, B]. Evidence gaps: [C]. Recommended preparation: [D]."*
- **Strictly Barred**: Consequential and normative assertions:
  *"You are suitable for this job."*  
  *"You have a 94% chance of success."*  
  *"Predictive lifetime employability score: 820."*

### Invariant 4: Strict Non-Consequential AI Determination (Clauses N22.19, N22.46–N22.47, N22.170–N22.174)
1. **Human Decision Sovereign**: Autonomous AI agents cannot independently accept binding legal contracts, negotiate compensation, execute financial transactions, submit applications without learner consent, reject applicants, or render hiring/admissions verdicts.
2. **Explicit Action Authorization**: Any automated agent action on behalf of a participant requires an explicit, revocable, cryptographic authorization token (`OpportunityAgentAction`).

### Invariant 5: No Fabricated Evidence & Disclosed AI Assistance (Clauses N22.26–N22.28, N22.176–N22.177)
1. An AI system generating an artifact (code, document, design) does not grant human capability or experience to the user.
2. All material AI contributions to learner portfolio artifacts must be transparently declared and verified via the Evidence Provenance Engine.

### Invariant 6: Sovereign Human Capability Passport & Selective Disclosure (Clauses N22.33–N22.37, N22.125)
1. The learner maintains absolute sovereign ownership of their **Human Capability Passport**.
2. Opportunity providers receive only purpose-bound, learner-consented selective disclosures.
3. Private learning dialogues, formative struggle telemetry, educator notes, and unrelated history remain strictly decoupled and unshared.

### Invariant 7: Zero Pay-to-Win Matching & Anti-Monopoly (Clauses N22.114–N22.117, N22.192–N22.193)
1. Matching relevance is purely a function of verified capability alignment and explicit learner preferences.
2. Financial payments, sponsorships, or premium subscriptions cannot artificially boost organic capability alignment scores or suppress qualified peers.
3. Sponsored listings must be explicitly and indelibly flagged.

### Invariant 8: Minor Safeguards & Vulnerable Learner Protections (Clauses N22.65–N22.70)
Opportunities involving minor learners undergo enhanced jurisdictional compliance, parental/institutional consent gating, strict contact brokering, and immediate suppression of unvetted commercial outreach.

## 3. The 12 Canonical Opportunity Archetypes

Milestone N22 governs 12 distinct opportunity archetypes across the lifelong learning spectrum:
1. `LEARNING`: Structured educational programs, courses, and workshops.
2. `PROJECT`: Collaborative or individual applied problem-solving challenges.
3. `MENTORSHIP`: Governed human-to-human guidance and expert review.
4. `INTERNSHIP`: Supervised organizational immersion with clear learning objectives.
5. `APPRENTICESHIP`: Regulated competency-based vocational development.
6. `RESEARCH`: Scholarly, scientific, or technological open inquiry.
7. `VOLUNTEER`: Civic, non-profit, or community-based contribution.
8. `FREELANCE`: Independent, scoped task or contract deliverables.
9. `EMPLOYMENT`: Governed salaried or wage-based professional roles.
10. `ENTREPRENEURSHIP`: Incubator, startup venture, or innovation backing.
11. `RESKILLING`: Targeted capability pivoting for evolving economic domains.
12. `FELLOWSHIP`: Selective research, artistic, or leadership development residency.

## 4. Opportunity Trust, Verification & Audit Infrastructure

1. **Provider Verification**: Opportunity providers must be verified via organizational registration, domain identity, and historical integrity signals.
2. **Fraud & Anomaly Reporting (Clause N22.155)**: A transparent, non-accusatory review workflow handles fraudulent postings, exploitative compensation, bait-and-switch requirements, or suspicious credential demands.
3. **Immutable Audit Ledger**: Every exchange request, disclosure grant, matching computation, and verification check generates an immutable audit record.
