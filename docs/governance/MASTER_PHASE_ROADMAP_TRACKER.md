## 🏛️ PART 1: Ongoing Operations & Continuous Governance (The Final Phase)

### 1.1 The Inviolable Core Non-Negotiables
Regardless of enterprise scale, district count, or valuation, the following four invariants hold permanently across all systems, models, and workflows:

1. **Human Authority > Algorithmic Optimization:** Mastery certification, consent mutation, role escalation, and safety incident dismissal remain permanently human-only. The AI recommends; qualified educators authorize.
2. **Demand-Gated Scaling:** No infrastructure module (billing, LMS synchronization, multi-tenant clustering) is activated ahead of verified, paying contractual demand. Unused complexity is treated as unused attack surface.
3. **Independent Verification > Internal Self-Certification:** Internal passing test suites (including 100% green CI badges) are never accepted as proof of child safety, cybersecurity integrity, or pedagogical efficacy. Third-party expert sign-offs are mandatory.
4. **Depth Before Breadth:** Every new subject, grade band, or sovereign jurisdiction must clear its own independent scope-lock and validation lifecycle before subsequent expansions are initiated.
