## 2. Scope of Regulatory Review

1. **Child Data Obligations:** Applicability of Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023 to the pilot's specific educational processing context.
2. **Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC):** Evaluation of whether the proposed OTP/SMS verification and school institutional representation legally satisfy verifiable consent rules.
3. **Behavioral Profiling Restrictions:** Verification that BKT pedagogical knowledge tracing ($P(L_t)$ belief updating) does not trigger statutory prohibitions against behavioral monitoring or tracking under Section 9(3).
4. **Third-Party AI Subprocessors:** Legal validity of Zero Data Retention (ZDR) addenda with cloud LLM providers under Indian data protection law.
5. **Breach Notification SLAs:** Compliance of incident response protocols with CERT-In 6-hour reporting mandates and Data Protection Board guidelines.

LEGAL OPINION SUMMARY:
