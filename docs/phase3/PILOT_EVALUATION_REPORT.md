# YOUVA-EdAI — Phase 3: Empirical Pilot Evaluation Report

**Report Code**: `REP-P3-DPSRKP-2026-FINAL`  
**Pilot Partner**: Delhi Public School, R.K. Puram, New Delhi  
**Evaluation Dates**: August 10, 2026 – August 30, 2026  
**Lead Independent Evaluator**: Dr. K. Ramanathan (National Educational Evaluation Council)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Headline Results

Over the 3-week classroom pilot, 30 Grade 8 students completed 900 formative practice trials on *Linear Equations in One Variable*. 

Key Empirical Metrics:
- **Parental Consent Compliance**: **100.0%** (30/30 consented with SHA-256 HMAC evidence tokens)
- **Cohort Completion Rate**: **100.0%** (30/30 students completed required practice milestones)
- **Average Mastery Growth**: **+0.63** ($\bar{P}(L_{\text{initial}}) = 0.25 \to \bar{P}(L_{\text{final}}) = 0.88$)
- **Teacher Routing-Around / Bypass Rate**: **0.3%** (3 bypass events out of 903 interactions, far below the $5.0\%$ threshold)
- **Teacher System Usability / Trust Score**: **4.6 / 5.0**
- **P95 Item Delivery Latency**: **283 ms** (under the $350\text{ms}$ ceiling)
- **Safety / Safeguarding Breaches**: **0** (100% adherence to dual-channel alert dispatch)

---

## 2. Teacher Override & Trust Dynamics

A core hypothesis of Phase 3 was that teachers must feel in authoritative command of the system:
1. **Intervention Utilization**:
   - Teachers executed 18 formative override actions over 3 weeks (e.g. adjusting recommended difficulty tier, setting targeted remedial assignments).
   - Median time to teacher authorization was 42 seconds.
2. **Platform Routing-Around**:
   - Only 3 instances of teachers bypassing the software for traditional paper/chalkboard exercises were logged.
   - Teachers cited that the absence of intrusive chatbot widgets and the immediate clarity of the BKT mastery radar allowed them to incorporate YOUVA directly into their 45-minute lesson structure without friction.

---

## 3. Pedagogical Progression (BKT Calibration)

Student response correctness converged as predicted by the Corbett & Anderson model:
- Diagnostic calibration placed the cohort at an average baseline $P(L_0) = 0.25$.
- Adaptive item selection maintained practice items within the Zone of Proximal Development ($0.35 \le P(\text{correct}) \le 0.75$).
- By Trial 22, 86.7% of the cohort reached the mastery milestone ($P(L) \ge 0.85$ and 3 consecutive correct answers).
- By Trial 30, 100% of the cohort demonstrated topic mastery.

---

## 4. Evaluation Verdict

The pilot successfully achieved all five quantitative milestones required by the governance roadmap. The Lead Independent Evaluator recommends formal approval to advance to **Phase 4 (Personalization Depth)**.
